Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective Date: 30.08.2025
Last Updated: 30.08.2025

Introduction

We understand that circumstances may arise requiring order modifications, and our cancellation and refund policy is structured to accommodate genuine customer needs while maintaining operational efficiency.

Order Cancellation Guidelines

Customers may request order cancellations exclusively during the pre-dispatch phase of order processing. Once an order has been handed over to our courier and officially shipped from our facility, cancellation requests cannot be accommodated due to logistical constraints and the irreversible nature of the shipping process.

Refund Processing and Timeline

For eligible cancellation requests that meet our criteria, refunds will be processed and initiated within 5-7 working days from the date of cancellation confirmation. All refunds are credited directly to the original payment method utilized during the initial checkout process, ensuring secure and traceable transactions.

Please note that while we process refunds promptly, your banking institution or payment service provider may require additional time to reflect the credited amount in your account, which is beyond our control.

Exchange & Replacement Policy

At Inkaura, we are committed to ensuring your complete satisfaction with every purchase. Our comprehensive exchange and replacement policy addresses various scenarios to provide you with confidence and peace of mind throughout your shopping experience.

Eligibility Criteria for Exchanges

Our exchange program accommodates several specific circumstances to ensure customer satisfaction. Exchanges are available when products are received in damaged condition, exhibit manufacturing defects, or when incorrect items have been shipped due to fulfillment errors.

Additionally, we recognize that size-related concerns are common in online shopping, and therefore accommodate exchanges for instances where an incorrect size was ordered or when the received product does not fit as expected based on our sizing specifications.

Request Submission and Timeline

To ensure optimal resolution of your concerns, all exchange requests must be initiated within 48 hours of the confirmed delivery date. This timeframe allows for immediate inspection and prompt communication of any issues.

To begin the exchange process, please contact our dedicated customer service team at inkauraa@gmail.com, providing your complete order details and a comprehensive description of the issue encountered.

This Cancellation & Refund Policy is effective as of 30.08.2025 and applies to all orders placed with Inkaura.
