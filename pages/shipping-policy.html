Shipping Policy

Effective Date: 30.08.2025
Last Updated: 30.08.2025

Introduction

At Inkaura, we are committed to providing a seamless and reliable shipping experience for all our customers. Our comprehensive shipping policy ensures your orders are processed efficiently and delivered safely to your designated address.

Order Processing and Fulfillment

Upon successful order confirmation, our dedicated fulfillment team initiates the processing of your order without delay. While we maintain streamlined operations to ensure prompt dispatch, processing times may occasionally vary based on product availability and inventory levels. We are committed to dispatching all confirmed orders at the earliest possible opportunity, keeping you informed throughout the process to ensure transparency and reliability.

Delivery Services and Coverage

Your order will be carefully packaged and shipped to the delivery address specified during the checkout process. Please note that delivery timelines are subject to variation based on your geographical location, local logistics infrastructure, and prevailing conditions that may affect transportation services.

Shipping Charges and Coverage

Our shipping charge policy reflects our commitment to providing value to our customers across different purchasing channels. For orders placed directly through the official Inkaura website, we are pleased to offer complimentary shipping services, with all associated charges fully covered by us. However, for orders processed through third-party e-commerce platforms, additional shipping charges may be applicable as determined by the respective platform's terms and conditions, over which we have limited control.

Order Tracking and Communication

Once your order has been dispatched from our fulfillment center, you will receive comprehensive confirmation along with detailed tracking information through email and SMS notifications. Our tracking system enables you to monitor your order's journey in real-time, providing regular updates on its status until successful delivery is confirmed at your designated address.

This Shipping Policy is effective as of 30.08.2025 and applies to all orders placed with Inkaura.
